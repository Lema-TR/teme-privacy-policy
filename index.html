Teme Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Teme ("Teme", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Teme mobile application.

Teme is an educational application designed to provide students with educational notes, questions, exercises, and related learning resources.

Account Deletion

If you want to delete your Teme account and the personal information associated with your account, you can request account deletion by contacting us at:

Email: temeeducations@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Teme account, or include the email address associated with the account in your request so that we can identify the account.

Your request should clearly state that you want to delete your Teme account and associated personal data.

Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted?

When an account deletion request is processed, Teme will delete the personal information associated with the account that is maintained for the operation of the Teme service, including applicable account information such as the user's name, email address, phone number, profile information, and authentication account.

Profile photos stored locally on the user's device are not uploaded to Teme's servers by the current version of the application. Users can remove locally stored profile photos by removing them through the application or from their device.

Data that may be retained

If certain information must be retained for legitimate reasons such as security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or regulatory compliance, that information may be retained for the period required for that purpose.

Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed after the request is received and, where necessary, verified.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use Teme, we may collect and process information necessary to provide the application's features.

Account Information

Teme uses Firebase Authentication to provide account registration and login services. Depending on the information you provide when creating or updating your account, this may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account authentication information
  • Other profile information you choose to provide

Profile Photo

Teme may allow you to select a profile photo. In the current version of the application, the profile photo is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers by Teme.

Notification Information

Teme may use push notifications to provide application-related notifications. Notifications are implemented using Expo's notification services and the underlying platform notification infrastructure.

Depending on your device and operating system, notification services may process technical information required to deliver notifications to your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use information collected through Teme for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Providing access to Teme's educational features
  • Maintaining and operating the application
  • Providing requested notifications
  • Responding to user requests and support inquiries
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the application
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

3. Firebase

Teme uses services provided by Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication, to support account registration and authentication.

Information processed by Firebase is handled according to Google's applicable privacy practices and terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google Privacy & Terms

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Teme may send push notifications related to the application.

You can disable notification permissions through your device's operating system settings.

5. Advertising

The current version of Teme does not display third-party advertisements.

The application may display informational or promotional carousel content related to Teme itself. This content is not third-party advertising.

6. Analytics

The current version of Teme does not use third-party analytics services to track users' behavior or activity for analytics purposes.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate Teme, such as authentication and notification service providers.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, security, and safety of Teme, its users, or others.

8. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Teme against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as necessary to provide Teme's services, maintain user accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it may be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Teme is an educational application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

11. Your Choices

You may control certain permissions through your device settings, including notification permissions.

You may also request deletion of your account and associated data using the account deletion process described above.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Teme, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or a request to delete your account or data, contact us at:

Email: temeeducations@gmail.com
